# Content Messaging Framework (CMF) v3.0

**Ālo Labs canonical content architecture for product marketing sites.**  
Derived from evidence-backed synthesis (buyer psychology, CRO, StoryBrand, JTBD, PAS) and validated on the [Silver Bullet](https://sb.alolabs.dev) homepage revamp (2026-06).

Use this document when restructuring homepage copy, help-center narratives, or any public-facing product story. Pair with [`WORKSHEET-TEMPLATE.md`](WORKSHEET-TEMPLATE.md) per product.

---

## Meta-rule (non-negotiable)

> **Emotional activation → Rational validation → Decision facilitation**

Win System 1 (fast, emotional) before System 2 (analytical). Never lead with brand, features, or price. Never use urgency before value. Never present price before problem cost and transformation value.

**Schwartz awareness:** Cold traffic runs the full sequence. Warmer traffic may enter later (e.g. Product Aware → start at C6). Match entry point to audience temperature.

---

## Three acts

| Act | Components | Purpose |
|-----|------------|---------|
| **I — Emotional contract** | C1–C5 | Hook, pain, stakes, credibility, vision |
| **II — Rational case** | C6–C10 | Mechanism, proof, authority, objections, offer framing |
| **III — Decision architecture** | C11–C14 | Risk reversal, CTA, urgency, post-decision reinforcement |

---

## Strategic scaffold (9 components)

For short pages, ads, or compressed OSS installs:

1. Hook / Pattern interrupt  
2. Problem + pain identification  
3. Consequence amplification  
4. Transformation vision  
5. Unique mechanism  
6. Social proof stack  
7. Risk reversal  
8. Primary CTA  
9. Urgency catalyst (authentic only)

---

## Implementation specification (14 components)

### C1 — Pattern interrupt hook

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Awareness (pre-awareness) |
| **Communicates** | Reader-only headline naming their exact situation — not your product |
| **Placement** | First visible copy; gatekeeper of entire page |
| **Length** | Headline 6–12 words; value prop ≤15 words |
| **Evidence** | Problem-first copy +27% vs solution-first (MarketingExperiments). Headline correlates r≈0.47 with conversion (Unbounce). |
| **Best practice** | Specific frustration > curiosity gap > bold claim. No vanity awards. No competitor-generic claims. |

**Checklist:** Names real situation · measurable outcome · not “leading platform” filler.

---

### C2 — Problem articulation (with identity framing)

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Awareness → Interest |
| **Communicates** | External problem + internal emotional cost in buyer’s language |
| **Placement** | Before any solution; establishes “this is for me” |
| **Structure** | Optional identity frame (“For [role] who [situation]”) + pain clusters as scannable cards |
| **Pull dimensions** | Functional gains unrealized · emotional confidence · social reputation |
| **Push dimensions** | Concrete pains the status quo causes (3–8 clusters typical) |
| **Evidence** | Voice-of-customer rewrites: 20–40% lift. Pain headlines: +49% CTR. |

**Best practice:** Source language from support tickets, reviews, sales calls — not marketing jargon.

---

### C3 — Consequence amplification (cost of inaction)

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Awareness (escalating severity) |
| **Communicates** | What worsens if they do nothing — financial, time, opportunity, emotional |
| **Placement** | Immediately after problem grid; problem = wound, consequence = infection spreading |
| **Evidence** | Losses weigh ~2–2.5× gains (Prospect Theory). Prevention framing stronger for >$50 B2B (Regulatory Focus). |
| **Best practice** | 1–2 quantified consequences where honest. Project factually — do not threaten. |

**Transition line:** Bridge to hope — “It doesn’t have to be [extreme ceremony] — but [high-risk work] needs a system.”

---

### C4 — Credibility bridge (earned authority)

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Interest |
| **Communicates** | Empathy + authority + method signal — brand as **guide**, buyer as **hero** |
| **Placement** | After pain arc; before transformation and solution |
| **Structure** | Empathy paragraph · authority (specific, honest) · how you’re different from “another prompt template” |
| **Evidence** | Authority before pain = self-promotion; after pain = reassurance (StoryBrand, Hovland-Weiss). |

**Best practice:** No fabricated customer counts. Alpha/honest stage disclosures beat fake logos.

---

### C5 — Transformational promise (vision of success)

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Interest → Desire |
| **Communicates** | Buyer-as-hero future in present tense — sensory, specific |
| **Placement** | After pain + credibility; contrast makes transformation meaningful |
| **Length** | 2–4 sentences micro-vision + optional expanded “day in the life” card |
| **Evidence** | Mental simulation of outcomes increases commitment (neuroscience). Toward + away motivation sequence (Pink, *Drive*). |

**Best practice:** “Closing laptop at 5pm knowing pipeline is forecasted” > “get your process under control.”

---

### C6 — Unique mechanism / differentiated solution

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Consideration |
| **Communicates** | **Category** + **unique mechanism** + transformation-first overview (not feature catalog) |
| **Placement** | After desire; before proof stack |
| **Structure** | Category sentence · named mechanism · 3–5 step “how it works” · honest results bounds |
| **Evidence** | Concrete metaphors +58% vs abstract features (Alter & Oppenheimer). Cognitive fluency failure −43% conversion. |

**Best practice:** Name what you do **not** do. Technical founders: buyer-accessible language only.

---

### C7 — Social proof / evidence stack

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Consideration → Evaluation |
| **Communicates** | Real evidence — not decoration |
| **Placement** | After mechanism; before pricing/commitment |
| **Proof types** | Enforcement grids · artifact trails · workflow tables · open source · version badges · honest benchmarks |
| **Deferred** | Customer logos, % improvement claims until real case studies exist |
| **Evidence** | 5+ reviews → 270% purchase likelihood (Northwestern). Contextual proof 3–5× footer testimonials. |

---

### C8 — Authority amplification (third-party validation)

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Evaluation |
| **Communicates** | Independent validation — press, analysts, integrations |
| **Placement** | After peer proof (C7) |
| **Best practice** | One meaningful mention > twenty generic logos |

*May merge into C7 for early-stage OSS products.*

---

### C9 — Objection neutralization

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Evaluation → Decision |
| **Communicates** | Top 3–5 objections in prospect’s words + direct answers |
| **Placement** | Late evaluation; FAQ or “Not for you” blocks |
| **Common objections** | “Too much process” · “We already use X” · “Another install” · “Won’t work for our stack” |
| **Evidence** | 70%+ cart abandonment from unaddressed objections (Baymard). |

**Optional C9b — Not for you:** Disqualify bad-fit buyers to increase trust.

---

### C10 — Pricing, value framing, tier architecture

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Decision |
| **Communicates** | Offer structure anchored to value already established |
| **Placement** | After proof; before risk reversal |
| **OSS note** | Often compressed: “Free / open source” + alpha disclosure |

**Rule:** Value-of-outcome anchor before price reveal. ≤3 tiers (Paradox of Choice).

---

### C11 — Risk reversal / guarantee

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Decision |
| **Communicates** | What makes trying safe — fail-closed gates, no lock-in, reversible install |
| **Placement** | Adjacent to offer/install |
| **Evidence** | +31% average lift with explicit guarantees (MarketingExperiments). |

**OSS patterns:** Open source · no telemetry · host-agnostic · planning artifacts stay in repo.

---

### C12 — CTA architecture

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Action |
| **Communicates** | One primary action + secondary paths |
| **Placement** | Hero (secondary), install section (primary), conviction moments throughout |
| **Best practice** | First-person or action-specific verbs. Remove nav on dedicated landing pages when appropriate. |

---

### C13 — Urgency / scarcity catalyst

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Action (acceleration) |
| **Communicates** | Authentic “why now” — real version, real cohort, real consequence of delay |
| **Placement** | **Last** before final CTA — never in hero |
| **Rule** | Manufactured countdown damages trust (73% detect artificial urgency). |

*Often omitted for low-consideration OSS install.*

---

### C14 — Post-decision reinforcement

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Funnel** | Post-action |
| **Communicates** | Affirm decision · micro-win · next steps |
| **Placement** | Thank-you, onboarding email, help “getting started” |
| **Evidence** | Reduces refund/churn 10–30% when dissonance addressed (Festinger). |

---

## Compression rules (low-consideration / OSS)

| Audience | Treatment |
|----------|-----------|
| Engineering leaders | Lead stakes, cost thesis, risk cards |
| IC developers | Lead daily pain, mechanism, retrieval/stack depth |
| OSS plugin install | Merge C5–C7 across solution sections; compress C8–C10 into callouts + install |

**Silver Bullet reference:** Components 1–4 full · 5–7 merged · 8–10 compressed into callouts/install.

## What most CMFs get wrong

1. **Brand-first hero** — Fix: C1–C2 before product name matters.  
2. **Urgency before value** — Fix: C13 only after C7–C11.  
3. **Decorative testimonials** — Fix: proof adjacent to claims.  
4. **Price before value** — Fix: C3 + C5 + C7 before C10.  
5. **No post-conversion reinforcement** — Fix: C14 in onboarding.

## Workflow for a product revamp

1. Research product repo (`README`, `PRD`, skills, hooks, tests).  
2. Fill [`WORKSHEET-TEMPLATE.md`](WORKSHEET-TEMPLATE.md) (or copy [`examples/silver-bullet-worksheet.md`](examples/silver-bullet-worksheet.md)).  
3. Map components → `site/index.html` sections and nav anchors.  
4. Apply Ālo site kit (`DESIGN.md`, `kit/`, `scripts/bootstrap-alo-site.sh`).  
5. Run `scripts/apply-site-chrome.py` and `scripts/validate-kit.sh`.  
6. Invoke skill **`alo-site-revamp`** for end-to-end agent execution.
